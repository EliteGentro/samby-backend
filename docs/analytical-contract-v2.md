# Analytical implementation contract, v2

The worker executes real computations from each immutable submitted workspace snapshot. The calculation version is `samby-daily-v2`. Saved v1 artifacts remain readable; comparisons reject different calculation contracts. Result construction does not mutate the current workspace or its source records.

## Forecast engines and evaluation

Naïve repeats the most recent eligible product/scope observation. Seasonal naïve repeats an explicitly selected complete prior season, default seven days. Both retain their source history and date coverage. LightGBM and CatBoost execute installed local regressors on the supplied data, including business-mode data; no precomputed prediction is substituted. Synthetic demo inputs remain explicitly labeled.

Advanced eligibility requires at least 56 consecutive observed daily quantities ending immediately before the selected forecast start. Missing days are not zero-filled. The selected product, location or declared inventory pool must have compatible units. The current implementation uses the latest consecutive segment, capped at 730 days, with lags of 1, 7 and 14 days, seven- and fourteen-day rolling means, weekday/month sine and cosine features, and elapsed calendar days. Saved history additionally flags supplied zero-stock observations from serviceObservations with their exact date, location, observed phase and source reference. These flags do not change the original sold quantities or model inputs and do not infer a full-day outage. All drivers are derived from strictly earlier demand or known calendar dates. Prices, promotion, availability and outside variables are not inferred. The UI identifies these drivers and the saved artifact records them.

Each advanced run fits a final model on all eligible pre-start history, then generates a recursive nonnegative forecast for the exact requested daily horizon. A separate chronological evaluation holds out the final 14 observed dates. Its model fits only the earlier training segment and predicts the whole held-out interval recursively, without feeding actual held-out targets back into features. Naïve and seven-day seasonal benchmarks use that same holdout. Tests change only held-out targets and verify that predictions remain unchanged while errors change. Tests also execute both installed model packages on two differently scaled business datasets.

Parameters are deterministic and deliberately small: 120 trees/iterations, learning rate 0.05, depth 4, one worker thread and seed 42. LightGBM uses 15 leaves and minimum five observations per child. CatBoost explicitly uses Ordered boosting and time-order preservation. No hyperparameter optimization, calibrated probability interval or hierarchical reconciliation is claimed. Exact installed package versions are retained in each result. Native macOS LightGBM requires the installed OpenMP runtime.

Diagnostics include evaluated dates, training cutoff, observations, feature names and actual-versus-predicted values. MAE is mean absolute error; RMSE is the square root of mean squared error; bias is mean prediction minus actual, so positive means overforecast. WAPE is 100 times total absolute error divided by total absolute actual quantity and is unavailable for an all-zero denominator. Baseline diagnostics require enough consecutive history for their complete holdout. No random historical split is used.

Primary package references: [LightGBM regressor](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/latest/pythonapi/lightgbm.LGBMRegressor.html), [CatBoost regressor](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/concepts/python-reference_catboostregressor), [CatBoost training parameters](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/references/training-parameters/common).

## Inventory and explicit policy

Every simulation uses 1–365 inclusive daily dates. Daily order is receipts, fulfillment of prior backorders, then new requested demand. Opening backorders require acceptance and an explicit relationship to reservations. Only reservations declared to belong to backorders may be released for that backlog. Opening backlog has unknown age and prior invoicing, so its fulfillment is not invoiced again. New unmet demand is either lost or carried according to the selected policy. Carried demand retains its modeled creation date and is served FIFO; later fulfillment counts once toward newly fulfilled sales and gross margin. Initial fill rate remains measured at the original requested day. Lost-margin calculations use lost units, excluding carried demand.

An owner can supply one dated purchase or a fixed-quantity reorder rule. A rule orders at daily close when available stock plus future ordered receipts minus backorders is at or below the explicit threshold. MOQ and case-pack constraints apply. The supplied lead time determines receipts, with a next-day minimum for zero-day rules under the stated daily convention. Safety stock and service targets produce measurements against the selected plan; they do not optimize it. Discounts change selling value and margin without inferred demand elasticity. Rule-generated order commitments enter the purchasing budget once at order date, separately from cash payments.

Purchase destination locations filter receipts to an explicit location or declared pool. An unallocated open purchase blocks restricted scope until its destination is supplied; an all-supplied aggregate remains available. Comparisons and pinned forecast inputs reject quantity-unit changes after standardization, even if product identity and dates match. Prior series are never silently converted. Pool membership is captured in the run snapshot and provenance.

## Financial propagation and incomplete records

Historical financeEvents observations are validated against the relevant receivable, provider-pending or payable stage and never enter projected cash a second time. Current financial records retain the existing linked-invoice/provider and purchase/payable reconciliation. Selected agreed or explicitly accepted proposed payment terms provide the contractual starting event and payment delay, optional advance percentage and event-relative date. Proposed terms do not imply agreement. An unrecorded advance percentage remains unknown; deriving the schedule requires recorded advance terms (including confirmed 0%) or an explicit no-advance scenario acceptance. Purchase and invoice dates remain distinct from receipt/fulfillment dates.

A new purchase creates a separate planned obligation. Changing a recorded purchase replaces its linked unpaid cash schedule exactly once and preserves its recorded paid allocation. Planned obligations remain separate from confirmed supplier External Debt. An optional accepted full-payment-before-dispatch prerequisite requires an exact dispatch date and rejects an incompatible payment schedule.

New fulfilled demand creates customer invoice cohorts only when explicitly declared incremental. Opening backlog creates no second invoice. Additional dated credit sales can create receivables without inventory. The selected unpaid fraction remains an explicit scenario assumption, never an inferred default probability. Advances reduce the cohort once; pre-invoice receipts are not added again when the invoice is recognized. Dates before opening require an explicit already-paid allocation. Events beyond the horizon remain outstanding. A selected outgoing payment can instead move to an accepted hypothetical exact date without creating another obligation.

Cash requires its reviewed opening boundary, category coverage and a usable in-window payment or collection after assumptions are applied. A debt-only projection makes no absolute cash claim. Identifiable pending financial records with unknown amount/allocation stay outside all numerical sums, retain their names in material warnings, and prevent contradictory absence declarations. A new-sales-only debt projection identifies the missing current receivable register and does not claim a total business balance.

## Persistence, access and view contract

Analytical requests use the shared workspace credential headers and optional account token. Viewer controls are disabled; finance roles cannot create inventory or demand forecasts, and inventory/buyer roles cannot create cash/debt output. The server enforces these permissions as well.

Jobs run independently of the browser. New submissions use idempotency keys; original/current reruns create new runs and preserve originals. Results retain exact dated series, event trace, assumptions, source snapshots, diagnostics and pinned baseline deltas. The 2D results and bounded saved scene manifests are complete; the optional 3D renderer remains deferred as explicitly permitted by §16.9 and acceptance scenario A27.
